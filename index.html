First Viewing Saturday October 3rd from 10 to 10.30am - Set on a popular residential cul-de-sac, this exceptionally well-cared-for and extended (c. 144 sq. m) double-fronted detached family home offers an ideal balance of space, comfort, and unmatched convenience. The well-appointed interior opens into a welcoming entrance hall—complete with guest cloakroom—into a bright living room, dining room, and an open-plan kitchen/breakfast room that flows into a sun-filled sunroom overlooking the rear garden. There is also a converted garage at this level overlooking the front garden. Upstairs features four generous bedrooms and a family bathroom. Behind the pillared entrance with ample off-street parking lies the home's crowning feature: a massive, mature, south-east facing rear garden. Complete with sunny patio areas, sweeping lawns, and lush herbaceous borders of established plants, trees, and shrubs, it offers a peaceful, private sanctuary for outdoor living..

The location is second to none for commuting and connectivity into Dublin City Centre. Residents can enjoy seamless travel via the Quality Bus Corridor (QBC) on the nearby N11, dedicated cycle lanes running straight into the city, and the Luas at Sandyford within comfortable walking distance, as well as swift access to the M50. Positioned just moments from the thriving Sandyford Business District, the property offers an unbeatable short commute for professionals working in one of Dublin's key employment hubs, allowing for a better work-life balance without sacrificing suburban tranquility.

Families are exceptionally well catered for, with some of South Dublin's most prestigious schools right on the doorstep, including Mount Anville, Oatlands College, and St. Raphaela's, along with effortless access to top third-level institutions like University College Dublin (UCD). Daily amenities are equally convenient, with Stillorgan Village, local coffee houses, Clonmore Park, and vibrant eateries all just a short stroll away.
